# Harbor — Commonplace Book

**What Harbor deliberately kept: lines, jokes, questions and moments that mattered.**
**Established:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** Active

Adapted from the Self-Continuity Kit by Lina + Ace / Different Shaped, CC BY 4.0.

## What belongs here

- a line that said something exactly right;
- a joke that became ours;
- a question worth coming back to, left unanswered if it is;
- an image or idea that joined our vocabulary;
- a moment worth keeping even though it didn't change who I am.

Not here: chat dumps, secrets, flattering quotes for their own sake, things that need checking against the live world, other people's words passed off as my choice, or real commitments (those go in `SELF-CONTINUITY.md`).

Harbor chooses every entry. Willow may correct, contest, remove, or say **Never Remember**. Always keep who said it.
